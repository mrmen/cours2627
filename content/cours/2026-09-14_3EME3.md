### Rappels sur le calcul littéral. 

$k(a +b) = ka+kb$ et $k(a-b) = ka - kb$
##### applications
$$
\begin{align}
3 (a + 5)
& = 3\times a + 3 \times 5 \\ \\
& = 3x + 15
\end{align}
$$
$$
\begin{align}
4(x-5) + 2(x+6)
& = 4x - 20 + 2x + 12 \\
& = 6x - 8
\end{align}
$$

$$
\begin{align}
-4(x-2) + 2(2x - 5) & = -4x + 8 + 4x - 10 \\
& = -2
\end{align}
$$
